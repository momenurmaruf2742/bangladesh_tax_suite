Bangladesh Tax Suite

Production Ready Tax Management System

1. Project Overview

Bangladesh Tax Suite is a modern Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform designed to streamline tax management for individuals and businesses in Bangladesh. It serves as a comprehensive tax lifecycle management system, covering everything from taxpayer registration and profile management to tax declaration, calculation, and payment integration.

The platform is built with a robust, scalable architecture and a modern technology stack, ensuring high performance, security, and maintainability.
